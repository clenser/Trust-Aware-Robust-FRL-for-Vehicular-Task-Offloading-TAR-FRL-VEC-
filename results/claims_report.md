## 14. Effect of the state-representation change (post-hoc; both runs retained)

The first full run (`run_20261022_215233_env_v2_protocol`) used road-length-normalised RSU distances (17 features).  Its failure log showed that a large share of the learned policy's failures were 'out of coverage', i.e. the policy had not learned RSU coverage.  The state was then changed to coverage-relative distances plus in-coverage flags (20 features).  **This change was made after seeing the first run's results**, so it is a design iteration, not a pre-registered one.
